## Key literature
Citation keys as given in the proposal; the notes are the user's summaries. (src: PROPOSAL.md:27-35)
- ilic2023: overview of intelligent systems in e-learning; conversational agents may raise motivation and engagement; personalisation as key strength.
- bianchi2004: dictionary-based conversational system assembling answers from pre-selected phrases and syntactic rules, plus an ontology-based knowledge component.
- griol2014: chatbot for elementary school students with speech input; replies from a pre-selection; judged very effective by domain experts.
- procter2017: master's thesis on a conversational e-learning system, focus on real-time user data rather than interaction mechanics.
- zhou2024, shishido2023: conversational systems for English as a foreign language, not truly interactive (pronunciation-error feedback only; spoken feedback to a spoken multiple-choice answer).
